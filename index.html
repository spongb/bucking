ⓘ How to use this trainer

Goal: Buck (cut) the stem into logs that maximize total value based on the AHMI grading matrix. Grade is determined by the small-end diameter and number of clear (defect-free) faces in each log.

  • Click anywhere on the stem diagram to place a cut.
  • Drag a cut marker left or right to reposition it.
  • Right-click a cut marker to remove it.
  • Roll Up / Roll Down (or arrow keys) rotates the stem so you can inspect all four faces for defects.
  • Logs must meet a standard length (8, 10, 12, 14, or 16 ft) after subtracting trim allowance. Pieces too short to make 8 ft are waste.
  • Diameter is measured at the small end of each nominal log length.
  • Click Score This Stem to see your value, the optimal solution, and an explanation of any difference.

The Face Defect Map below the stem shows which faces each defect affects. Use it alongside the rotation view to decide where to cut.
